# credicare
An AI early-warning platform that detects financial distress up to 90 days in advance by tracking real-time spending, savings, and debt habits. Features a "What-If" financial simulator, explainable loan readiness, and adaptive debt recovery to help banks and users proactively prevent defaults.
CreditCare AI — Your Financial Health Companion

CreditCare AI is an intelligent financial-health platform designed to help individuals identify, prevent, and recover from financial distress before it becomes a crisis.

Instead of waiting for customers to miss EMIs or default on loans, CreditCare analyzes income, daily expenses, savings, debts, EMIs, spending behavior, and credit-health indicators to identify early signs of financial stress and provide personalized interventions.

🚀 Key Features

- Daily Expense Tracker — Tracks and categorizes income, expenses, savings, and debt payments.
- Spending Behavior Analysis — Identifies patterns such as increasing discretionary spending, declining savings, and rising debt pressure.
- Financial Health Score — Provides an understandable score with an explanation of the factors affecting it.
- Early-Warning System — Detects potential financial distress before it leads to missed payments or excessive debt.
- Loan Readiness Assessment — Evaluates whether a user appears financially ready for additional borrowing and explains the reasons.
- Loan Planning — Creates personalized and adaptable repayment plans based on income, expenses, savings, and existing obligations.
- What-If Simulator — Allows users to simulate scenarios such as taking a new loan, reducing income, or increasing expenses.
- Financial Digital Twin — Creates a dynamic representation of the user's overall financial situation.
- Debt Recovery Mode — Generates realistic, adaptive strategies for users already struggling with debt.
- Multiple Debt Simulator — Helps compare different debt-repayment strategies and their potential impact.
- Invisible Expense Detector — Identifies recurring spending patterns that may significantly affect long-term savings.
- Next-Best-Action Engine — Provides the most relevant financial action based on the user's current situation.
- AI Financial Coach — Provides personalized, explainable financial guidance.

🔄 How It Works

Track → Analyze → Predict → Warn → Personalize → Recover

CreditCare continuously updates the user's financial profile as their income, expenses, savings, and debt change. The AI then identifies risks, explains them clearly, and adapts recommendations to the user's changing financial situation.

🏦 Impact

For Customers

- Improve financial awareness and spending habits
- Manage debt more responsibly
- Make informed borrowing decisions
- Identify financial distress early
- Follow personalized recovery plans

For Banks

CreditCare enables banks to move from reactive intervention after default to proactive support before financial distress becomes a crisis.
